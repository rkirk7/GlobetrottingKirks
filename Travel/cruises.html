Cruises

Many people wonder why it's helpful to book a cruise through a travel agent. In addition to sharing our expertise in helping you choose the best cruise line and itinerary, we can ensure you are getting the best value (matching or beating public rates), provide perks such as onboard credit, and offer concierge service throughout the travel planning process, handling communications with the cruise line. Plus, our commission comes from the fare you're paying, so it's never an extra cost to you!
