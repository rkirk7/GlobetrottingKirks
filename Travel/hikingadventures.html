    Tell Me More About Active Adventures

    Do you like the thrill of adventure? We certainly do! The Kirk girls have made it their mission to hike and explore the most beautiful places in the world. Although there's still so much more to discover, some of our favorite adventure locales include:

    New Zealand

    We must love New Zealand, because we've both been there three times! There's a reason this country was chosen as the setting for Middle Earth; everywhere you look there's stunning scenery.

    Patagonia (Chile and Argentina)

    We've been here once and can't wait to go back. (In fact, the trip is already planned--now to find the time!) Between completing the multi-day W Trek in Chile's Torres Del Paine and conquering multiple rigorous hikes in Argentina's El Chalten/Glaciares National Park, there's so much beautiful scenery to soak in.

    The Himalayas (Nepal)

    For adventurers, Nepal is best known as the home to Mount Everest. There are so many astonishingly beautiful mountains here, though. We hiked the Annapurna Circuit over the course of ten challenging, wonderful days. Regan is dying to go back and do the Three Passes Trek. Anyone want to come along?
