Fabulous Hikes and Thrill-Seeking Around the World


    Do you like the thrill of adventure? We certainly do! The Kirk girls have made it their mission to hike and explore the most beautiful places in the world.

    Tell Me More About Active Adventures

    New Zealand

    We must love New Zealand, because we've both been there three times! There's a reason this country was chosen as the setting for Middle Earth; everywhere you look there's stunning scenery.

    Of course, there's so much else to do there as well, from cherry picking to white water rafting to a fabulous feast at Hobbiton.

    Patagonia (Chile and Argentina)

    We've been here once and can't wait to go back. (In fact, the trip is already planned--now to find the time!) Between completing the multi-day W Trek in Chile's Torres Del Paine and conquering multiple rigorous hikes in Argentina's El Chalten/Glaciares National Park, there's so much beautiful scenery to soak in.

    The Himalayas (Nepal)

    For adventurers, Nepal is best known as the home to Mount Everest. There are so many astonishingly beautiful mountains here, though. We hiked the Annapurna Circuit over the course of ten challenging, wonderful days. Regan is dying to go back and do the Three Passes Trek. Anyone want to come along?

    United States

    There are so many beautiful mountains and trails to be discovered in the U.S., and we certainly haven't gotten to all of them yet. However, we've been to some astonishing places!

    Switzerland

    The Alps run 750 miles through eight countries, with some 100 peaks above 13,000 feet. While the Swiss towns are nice, the Alps are spectacular. We enjoyed exploring three areas featuring Mont Blanc, Matterhorn, and the trio of Eiger, Monch and Jungfrau. Seeing the same mountain from different views was fascinating. With the efficient (though pricey) mountain trains and cable cars, access is easy for everyone, though the altitude is tougher to adapt to for some.
