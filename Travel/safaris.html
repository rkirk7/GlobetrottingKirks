Safari

Whenever we're asked our favorite type of travel, it's hard to come up with an answer. However, we've gone on safari more times than we've done anything else--which should tell you how much we love the experience. We specialize in safari trips and would love to help you plan your dream safari. Here are some of the great places we've been:

Botswana

We've been to Botswana twice. The Okavango Delta is such a special place; if you've seen a nature documentary about wildlife in Africa, there's a very good chance you saw a video of the delta! We've been privileged to have stunning animal encounters here, including a rare sighting of two cheetahs swimming across a river. Botswana is also the only place we've visited on safari where we had a female guide, part of an inspiring program to encourage more women to become guides!
