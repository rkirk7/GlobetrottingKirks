Now I'm excited to not only share my insider expertise and enthusiasm with clients, but also make their dreams come true, leveraging Virtuoso and Fora Travel's extensive industry resources and relationships.

Powered by Fora, my exceptional expertise, scrutiny, creativity, and communication skills produce show-stopping experiences that are unforgettable.

From cruises to safaris, tours of Europe to weekend getaways, I wholeheartedly embrace the research, planning, advocating, minutiae, and bookings. All you have to do is pack!

About Regan

I am an English professor and TaeKwonDo instructor near Orlando, Florida; in my past life, I was a corporate lawyer in New York City. I have a B.A. from the University of Washington in English and economics, a J.D. from Yale University, and an M.F.A. in creative writing from Columbia University. I also design custom iPhone and Android apps and have a professional coding certification from MITxEmeritus (plus, I made this website).

I travel often, constantly seeking out beautiful locales and outdoor adventures. (This picture is from the four-day W Trek in Torres Del Paine National Park in Chile.) Nothing brings me as much joy as encountering wildlife in its natural habitat, whether it's leopards in South Africa, sea turtles in the Galapagos, polar bears in Canada, or whales and penguins in Antarctica. My dream as a travel advisor is to help people experience incredible moments such as these for themselves.

I am an avid writer, with several novels and one musical in my repertoire, and when I was seven years old I won a national contest to invent and publish a board game. In the past, I've moonlit occasionally as a pianist at a local establishment. Two adorable goldendoodles keep me on my toes!
