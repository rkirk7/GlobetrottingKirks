The Emperor Penguin Encounter

Emperor penguins are the largest and rarest penguin species on Earth—and the hardest for travelers to see in the wild.

  • They live deep within Antarctic ice
  • They are absent from most cruise routes
  • Very few expeditions ever attempt access

For many explorers, seeing emperor penguins in the wild is the ultimate reason to travel to Antarctica.
