Important Notes About Antarctica

  • Best wildlife viewing: Mid-January through February is the best time to see penguin chicks and also is a great time to see whales and other marine mammals.
  • Expedition cruising at its finest: Expedition cruising is a very special kind of experience we love! The itinerary is fluid and the expedition team works tirelessly to ensure we get the best time on excursions, whether it’s kayaking with whales or walking with penguins.
  • Smaller ships matter: Smaller ships like the Eclipse II are better; only 100 people can be on the ground at a time, so a ship that holds 200 people or fewer is ideal to maximize your time.
  • The ultimate itinerary: Our favorite Antarctica itinerary is the Falkland Islands, South Georgia, and Antarctica. South Georgia is known as either the “Serengeti of the Poles” or “Galapagos of the South”; it’s home to hundreds of thousands of king penguins and one of the most incredible places we’ve ever been. That itinerary also has the benefit of only crossing the notorious Drake Passage once.
