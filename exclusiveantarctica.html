Important Notes About Antarctica

  • Best wildlife viewing: Mid-January to mid-February is peak time for penguin chicks, whales, and other marine mammals.
  • Expedition cruising at its finest: Flexible itineraries give you the best conditions for kayaking with whales, walking with penguins, and landing at pristine sites.
  • Smaller ships matter: Only 100 people can be ashore at once, so ships with 200 guests or fewer maximize your time on land.
  • The ultimate itinerary: Falklands, South Georgia & Antarctica, which allows you to experience the legendary wildlife of South Georgia (the "Galapagos of the Poles") and only one crossing of the Drake Passage.
