Spend the 2026 holiday season by the South Pole!

December 22, 2026 – January 6, 2027

This 16-day itinerary is the perfect way for your family to enjoy the holiday season in Antarctica. After you get yourself to Buenos Aires, almost everything is included (see below). We'll spend a night in a BA hotel, then catch a charter flight to Ushuaia, the southernmost town in the world. From there, we'll spend about one and a half days crossing the Drake Passage before arriving in Antarctica on either Christmas Eve or Christmas Day!

We expect to have ten full days on the continent, with excellent viewings of whales and a variety of penguins, such as gentoo, chinstrap, and Adelie. We'll also push into the remote Weddell Sea, where there's a small chance of seeing an emperor penguin! After ten days of zodiacs, kayaking, paddleboarding, polar plunging, helicoptering, submarining, and reveling in sheer luxury, we'll cross the Drake Passage again and leave with new, precious memories.

See king penguins and so much more on the ultimate Antarctica itinerary, with South Georgia and the Falkland Islands, on TWO separate sailings in early 2028

January 15, 2028 – February 3, 2028

OR

February 13, 2028 – March 3, 2028

This 20-day sailing is our absolute favorite itinerary. Everyone knows about Antarctica, but the secret gem is South Georgia, known as the "Galapagos of the Poles" or "Serengeti of the South", which contains the largest king penguin colonies in the world. After you get yourself to Buenos Aires, almost everything is included (see below). We'll spend a night in a BA hotel, then catch a charter flight to Ushuaia, the southernmost town in the world. From there, we'll set sail! Since this is an expedition, the itinerary is always subject to change, but here's the plan:

We'll spend a day at sea before arriving at the Falkland Islands, where a long excursion to Volunteer Point might let you walk amongst a large colony of king penguins (an experience the Globetrotting Kirks found utterly magical) or you might head out to see albatrosses and rockhopper penguins. We plan to spend two days there. Then two more days at sea brings us to majestic South Georgia, where we can kayak with king penguins, watching macaroni penguins porpoise, visit Earnest Shackleton's grave, and visit places like Fortuna Bay, where more than 100 thousand king penguins gather. After three days in South Georgia, it's time to head to the continent.

Two days at sea bring us to Antarctica, where we'll spend five days exploring. At this time of year, the penguin chicks should be small and goofy, and the marine mammals will be active. If conditions allow, we'll set foot on the continent (perhaps more than once!), in addition to numerous other activities. Hopefully, we'll sail through the Girlache Strait, with whales spouting all around. Finally, it will be time to cap off our trip with a two-day sail across the infamous Drake Passage back to Ushuaia. From there, we'll catch a charter flight back to Buenos Aires, where instead of "goodbye" we'll say "au revoir"--until we meet again!
