Warrior Camp Sponsored by Evermore



Welcome to the November 2025 Warrior camp, sponsored by Evermore Orlando Resort! This camp will be an opportunity for fun, sun, and sweat at Central Florida's only beach resort. Sign up today!

Who: Orange belts and above, as well as immediate family, are welcome

When: October 31st-November 2nd. Check-in is at 4 p.m. on Friday, but you're welcome to come early to enjoy the amenities. Check-out is at 10 a.m. on Sunday, but you are welcome to stay and enjoy the resort all day!

Where: Evermore Orlando Resort How Much: $350 covers:

  • Two nights in a shared room at the resort (single supplement available). Rooms will be located in the resort's beautiful vacation homes, which have their own pools, pool tables, full kitchen, TVs in every room, and massive recreation areas
  • Excellent TaeKwonDo clinics taught by a variety of certified instructors
  • Fun and games
  • A Warrior Camp t-shirt
  • Access to the Evermore's 8-acre lagoon and pool, along with beach loungers, water slide, kayaks, and stand up paddleboards. Go to the beach without ever leaving Central Florida!
  • Participation in Evermore's recreational activities (some, such as "make your own stuffed animal", may be subject to an extra materials fee)
  • Pizza night
  • A $50 gift card towards food at the Evermore's restaurants and food trucks. You're also welcome to bring your own food

To sign up, call your school owner today! The deadline to sign up and payment due date is October 8th, no exceptions. Don't miss what's sure to be an epic event!
