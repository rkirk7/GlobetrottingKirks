This hotel is an 8-minute walk to Disney Springs and comes with a complimentary shuttle to the Disney Parks (be sure to make a shuttle reservation). Kids 11 and under eat free at the restaurant on site (restrictions apply). Wi-fi is included. There is a heated swimming pool and a fitness center.
