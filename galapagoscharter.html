2027 16-Person Luxury Catamaran Charter in the Galapagos

From July 9-17, 2027, the Globetrotting Kirks, Mei-Mei and Regan, will escort a group of just 14 other travelers on an exclusive adventure in the Galapagos Archipelago

Here's the trip in detail

  • Who: Mei-Mei and Regan, plus 14 guests
  • What: Seven nights aboard the stunning Galapagos Elite catamaran, which we’ll have all to ourselves. There will be multiple stops each day with frequent opportunities for walks, hikes, snorkeling, kayaking, paddle boarding, and more.
  • Where: You’ll get yourselves to Quito, Ecuador, and from there we will fly to San Cristobal in the Galapagos Archipelago
  • When: July 9-17, 2027 (with the option of extending to the 20th on our favorite island)
  • Why: The Galapagos is at the top of many people’s bucket lists and there’s no better way to experience all the islands have to offer than on an intimate catamaran staffed by local experts. Journey stress-free with a small group of like-minded travelers, under the careful planning and attention of Mei-Mei and Regan.
