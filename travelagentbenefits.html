How Do Travel Advisors Make Money These Days?

When we help you book a hotel or cruise, we don't charge any fees—the hotel or cruise line pays--you don't! That’s because the public rate usually includes a commission fee the vendor pays to travel agencies after you travel. If you don't use a travel advisor, the vendor keeps that money—so you're paying for a service you're not getting! When we help you design a customized itinerary, we will charge a planning fee based on the length of your trip. In exchange for this fee, you'll get our full attention and expertise, a complete, seamless itinerary tailored for you, access to all of our resources and potential perks, all the necessary bookings, and ongoing care, communication and advocacy.
