    Important Notes About Antarctica

    • Wildlife: December is the start of the whale season, so whale sightings will be frequent. The penguins' eggs are ready to hatch and may have started to do so, so adult penguins abound.
    • Expedition cruising at its finest: Expedition cruising is a very special kind of experience we love! The itinerary is fluid and the expedition team works tirelessly to ensure we get the best time on excursions, whether it’s kayaking with whales or walking with penguins.
    • Smaller ships matter: Smaller ships like the Eclipse II are better; only 100 people can be on the ground at a time, so a ship that holds 200 people or fewer is ideal to maximize your time.
    • An exceptional itinerary: This itinerary is for the traveler who doesn't have time for (or has already been to) South Georgia (see our other Antarctica group for 2028). Instead, this one provides a full ten days in Antarctica--a rarity for expeditions! In addition to thoroughly exploring the continent, we will push into the remote Weddell Sea, where few visit. We expect to see many whales; stunning landscapes; and Adelie, chinstrap, gentoo penguins. If we're very lucky, in the Weddell Sea we might just spot a rare emperor penguin!
