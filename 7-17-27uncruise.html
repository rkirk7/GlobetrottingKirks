    Important Notes About Alaska

    • Wildlife: July is the start of peak season for wildlife sightings in Alaska, especially bears and humpback whales. In addition, we're likely to see bald eagles, sea lions, seals, sea otters, orcas, and more!
    • Expedition cruising at its finest: Expedition cruising is a very special kind of experience we love! The itinerary is fluid and the expedition team works tirelessly to ensure we get the best time on excursions.
    • Juneau roundtrip: Most Alaska cruises require a stop in Canada due to maritime law. UnCruise Adventures is one of only a few that can operate entirely within Alaska, maximizing your time.

    Itinerary Information

    Departure: July 17-24, 2027, (roundtrip from Juneau) on board the Wilderness Legacy

    See the sailing on UnCruise's site here.

    The Wilderness Legacy is a classic riverboat converted for expedition usage. Among her highlights are a comfortable lounge area, viewing opportunities around the ship on multiple decks, and the Sea Dragon, a custom platform designed for ease of getting in and out of kayaks and skiffs.

    The ship holds at most 86 guests, making it an intimate experience.
