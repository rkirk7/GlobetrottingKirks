Day-to-Day Itinerary

  • January 2, 2027: Arrive in Buenos Aires. Arrange your own transportation to the included hotel (or we can help arrange it for you).
  • January 3: Early morning transfer to the airport. Charter flight to Ushuaia. If conditions and timing permit, city tour and tour of beatiful Tierra del Fuego. Board the World Traveller and embark!
  • January 4: Sail across the Drake Passage, hoping for the "Drake lake" but prepared for the 25% possibility of the "Drake shake". Fortunately, your ship has the latest in modern technology in terms of stabilization. Get to know your expedition team and learn about what to expect in the upcoming days.
  • January 5: Day two of the Drake Passage. Arrive in Antarctica!
  • January 6-9: Explore the Antarctic Peninsula, with locations and activities at the discretion of the captain and the expedition team. Expect zodiac rides, walks on the continent, a polar plunge, and the potential for kayaking and/or camping (sign-ups in advance). Sightings should include many whales and penguins of several varieties, including gentoo, chinstrap, and Adelie.
  • January 10-12: Sail the XXX Sea towards South Georgia. Keep an eye out for whales along the way.
  • January 13-15: Explore South Georgia, again with locations and activities at the captain's and expedition team's discretion. Expect to see hundreds of thousands of king penguins, and potentially walk among them. A likely stop is Grytvyken, where you can mail postcards from the southernmost post office in the world and visit Ernest Shackleton's grave.
  • January 16-17: At sea.
  • January 18: Explore Port Stanley, the capital of the Falkland Islands. We strongly recommend the Volunteer Point excursion to see the king penguin colony and are happy to help arrange this for you (excursion not included in the cruise price). You can also explore the town, visit an albatross colony, or investigate other activities!
  • January 19: Expedition activities in the Falkand Islands at the captain's and expedition team's discretion.
  • January 20: At sea.
  • January 21: Arrive at Ushuaia. Transfer to the airport and charter flight to Buenos Aires, where your expedition ends. Consider an extension to Iguazu Falls or Patagonia while you're in Argentina!
