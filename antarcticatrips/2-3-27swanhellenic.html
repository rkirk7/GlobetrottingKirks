    Important Notes About Antarctica

    • February is a great time of year to visit, as you'll have warm (for Antarctica) weather, penguin chicks, and lots of marine mammals like whales.
    • Expedition cruising at its finest: Expedition cruising is a very special kind of experience we love! The itinerary is fluid and the expedition team works tirelessly to ensure we get the best time on excursions, whether it’s kayaking with whales or walking with penguins.
    • Smaller ships matter: Smaller ships like the SH Minerva are better; only 100 people can be on the ground at a time, so a ship that holds 200 people or fewer is ideal to maximize your time. This ship holds only 150 people.

    Itinerary

    (Note: Subject to change based on conditions or at the captain's discretion.)

    • February 2, 2027: Arrive in Buenos Aires.
    • February 3: Early morning transfer to the airport. Charter flight to Ushuaia. Board the SH Minerva and embark!
    • February 4: Sail across the Drake Passage, hoping for the "Drake lake" but prepared for the 25% possibility of the "Drake shake". Fortunately, your ship has the latest in modern technology in terms of stabilization. Get to know your expedition team and learn about what to expect in the upcoming days.
    • February 5: Day two of the Drake Passage. Arrive in Antarctica!
    • February 6-8: Explore the Antarctic Peninsula, with locations and activities at the discretion of the captain and the expedition team. Expect zodiac rides, walks on the continent, a polar plunge, and the potential for kayaking and other activities. Sightings should include many whales and penguins of several varieties, including gentoo, chinstrap, and Adelie.
    • February 9-10: At sea.
    • February 11: Peter Island, Bellinghausen Sea.
    • February 12-14: At sea.
    • February 15: Shepard Island.
    • February 16: At sea.
    • February 17: Bay of Whales.
    • February 18: Cross the International Dateline.
    • February 19: At sea.
    • February 20-26: Explore the Ross Sea.
    • February 27-28: Explore the Balleny Islands.
    • March 1-3: At sea.
    • March 4: Cruising Campbell Island.
    • March 5: Cruising Auckland Islands.
    • March 6: Cruising Snares Island.
    • March 7-8: Dunedin.
