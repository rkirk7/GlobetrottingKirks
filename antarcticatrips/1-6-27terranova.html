    Important Notes About Antarctica

    • Wildlife: January is in the early portion of the whale season, so whale sightings will be frequent. Chicks have begun to hatch at this time, so there should be both adult and baby penguins.
    • Expedition cruising at its finest: Expedition cruising is a very special kind of experience we love! The itinerary is fluid and the expedition team works tirelessly to ensure we get the best time on excursions, whether it’s kayaking with whales or walking with penguins.
    • Smaller ships matter: Only 100 people can be on the ground at a time, which means most expedition ships need to rotate groups for experiences. As a 98-person vessel, the XX is the ideal size.
