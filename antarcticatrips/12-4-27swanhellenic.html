    Important Notes About Antarctica

    • Time of year: December is the start of whale season. Adult penguins will be courting and nesting!
    • Expedition cruising at its finest: Expedition cruising is a very special kind of experience we love! The itinerary is fluid and the expedition team works tirelessly to ensure we get the best time on excursions, whether it’s kayaking with whales or walking with penguins.
    • Smaller ships matter: Smaller ships like the SH Vega are better; only 100 people can be on the ground at a time, so a ship that holds 200 people or fewer is ideal to maximize your time. The Vega holds just 150.

    What's the discount?

    Our rate starts at $9,950 per person. Meanwhile, the public, discounted rate for the same room starts at $11,375 per person. You'll also receive $250 in onboard credit for balcony rooms and above.
