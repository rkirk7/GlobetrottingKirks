    Have you dreamed of standing among emperor penguins ever since watching March of the Penguins? They're tough to reach; the vast majority of Antarctica expeditions will never see an emperor penguin. The best way to reach them by ship is on an expedition to Snow Hill island on the eastern side of the Antarctic peninsula, traveling by helicopter from the ship to within walking distance of the colony.

    This expedition isn't for the faint of heart. Success is not guaranteed--it's possible we'll go all that way and be unable to reach the island. But imagine being part of the tiny percentage of people to ever stand among these majestic birds. There are only a few sailings each year, across all of the expedition cruise lines, that will attempt this landing. And we've got a discount on this one, which is guaranteed to sell out!

    This expedition is recommended for: 1. people who have already been to Antarctica and now wish to experience the even more exclusive expedition to see the emperors, or 2. people who are passionate about emperor penguins and prioritize seeing them over everything else. If you don't fall into one of these categories, you may wish to considerone of our other Antarctica specials, and/or fill out our Antarctica interest form.
